Fade the Public

No real public-split data to report again this week — Action Network's numbers are PRO-gated everywhere we looked — so here's what we can say for certain from the lines themselves. Arizona's number against the Rams has moved nearly three points since the week opened, after a season-ending setback for their starting quarterback forced a rookie into his first career start, and the Rams' top receiver landed on injured reserve in the same stretch — the market's clearly already pricing in both, which is part of why we're not backing either side's spread this week despite a real situational angle underneath it. Green Bay's number over Minnesota barely budged even after their own lead back was ruled out for surgery, which says the market isn't worried about the committee behind him the way you might expect. And Baltimore actually grew as a favorite over the Chargers after losing their starting quarterback for the rest of the season — a sign the market trusts the backup and the run game there more than the name at quarterback.
