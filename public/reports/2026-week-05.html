Week 5 Market Report

Most of the handicappers we track hadn't published for Week 5 as of our usual pull — paywalled, gone quiet, or just not posted yet this cycle — so there's no two-source consensus to lead with this week. What we do have is a real find in our own numbers: Houston was a modest road favorite last week and is a much bigger one now, but the market already has them as underdogs again two weeks from now — exactly the kind of look-ahead spot that has quietly beaten the spread more often than not for whoever's catching that favorite, and it's Tennessee doing the catching this week. We checked whether that same spot supports taking Tennessee on the moneyline too — it doesn't; the numbers say this is a spread play, not a team to win outright. Beyond that, Saquon Barkley's hamstring has reopened Philadelphia's backfield, Baltimore's night could turn on whether Lamar Jackson's ankle lets him suit up, and two still-winless teams — Houston and Tennessee — play each other looking for win number one. A deep run of player props rounds out the board.

Last Week's Track Record

Derrick Henry, Saquon Barkley, Ja'Marr Chase (who left early with a concussion) and Jahmyr Gibbs (downgraded to doubtful pregame, capped workload) came up short. Worth flagging plainly: two of those picks weren't real handicapping misses at all — they were ours. The Chiefs' and Seahawks' running back props that week were built on stale team assumptions for two players who'd actually changed teams before the season even started, so neither one suited up for the game the pick named. We've added a roster check (every player prop now gets verified against their actual current team before it's used) so that doesn't happen again, and we're tracking those two as voided, not as losses, in our running results.
