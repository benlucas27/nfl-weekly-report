New-stadium debuts & the total — Bills' Highmark Stadium opener vs. Lions Data angle

Buffalo opened Highmark Stadium hosting Detroit this week — the 19th ground-up new-stadium debut since 2000. Two ways to slice the history:

Debut game only (n=18 scored) 9–9 over/under — a coin flip, no edge
Full debut season, all home games (n=143 scored) 62 over – 81 under (57% under) — a real lean toward the under across the bigger sample

Lead rusher in the debut game: averaged +5.0 yards above their own season baseline, hitting above it in exactly half of instances (n=18) — no usable signal either way. Read this as: the "new stadium magic" theory holds up a little for full-season unders, not at all for a single-game rushing pop. Two of the 19 instances (LV/LA 2020) were played with no fans due to COVID — a real confound worth knowing about, not excluded from the numbers above.

Home team favored by 10+ → lean the home RB's rushing yards Data angle

Run via historical_screen.py --scan, which screens 42 structural cohorts across 1999–2026 and keeps only the ones with a real edge over their own baseline (not a flat 50%). The closing line itself barely moves for any of these — Vegas already prices rest, weather, and spread size — but game-script volume shifts hard and consistently, which is exactly what a rushing/passing yardage prop needs:

Home team favored by 10+ (n=707): rushing yards +16.1% vs. baseline, carries +9.9% — the trailing team's RB rushing yards −13.5%
Total line set at 50+, i.e. an expected shootout (n=663): passing yards +21% (home) / +23% (away) — lean the passing props up, fade pure rushing volume, when this week's total is set unusually high
