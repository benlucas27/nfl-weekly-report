Lions @ Bills

Buffalo christens Highmark Stadium on Thursday night against a Lions team that matches up with them about as well as anyone left on the schedule. This one's being treated as a springboard game for both sides, and the number reflects it — close enough that the public money will be split, but the line has crept toward Buffalo through the week.

The Matchup

This is the kind of spot where the crowd noise actually matters — Buffalo's new building holds fewer people than the old one but keeps them closer to the field, and the Bills have leaned into that all week in how they've talked about the opener. Detroit's the better team on paper in a lot of metrics, but they're walking into a genuinely hostile building for the first time this season, and that tends to show up early before a team settles in.

Buffalo's approach when they're favored at home hasn't changed in years: run it, protect the ball, make Detroit's defense play from behind. That's the backbone of both the spread pick and the Cook prop above — it's not a exotic read, it's just what tape and history both say happens when this team is in this spot.

Fade the Public
