Week 2 Consensus Report

Buffalo's new building christened itself Thursday night; the rest of the week is a fairly ordinary slate with two real standouts — Philadelphia walking into Nashville as a near-two-touchdown road favorite, and Miami catching the worst possible matchup at Levi's Stadium. Full slate below, but the parlay-worthy stuff is short on purpose.

This Week's Best Bets

Only legs worth putting in a parlay. Every other game is covered below with no pick attached, on purpose.

  • PHI Eagles −7 at Tennessee High Confidence
    This is about as lopsided as Week 2 gets — Philadelphia is the better team by a wide margin, Tennessee's defense has struggled to slow anyone on the ground, and the market's fully priced that in without any pushback.
  • Saquon Barkley — Over rushing yards High Confidence
    He's been the focal point of that offense all year, Tennessee's run defense is one of the softer matchups on the board, and a big road favorite like this leans on the ground game even more than usual.
  • SF 49ers −13.5 vs Miami Confident
    The number is enormous, but so is the talent gap — this is the kind of spot where San Francisco leans on the run early and often to put it away.
  • Washington @ Dallas — Over 50.5 Confident
    The total here is set unusually high, which is normally the market telling you both offenses are expected to move the ball — that tends to be a better read than fading it.
  • DEN Broncos vs Jacksonville Confident
    Early-season trips to altitude have historically been a rough assignment for visiting teams before they've adjusted — not a blowout lean, just a real, if modest, home edge.
